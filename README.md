## 🔭 Current Projects

### Mwanachuoshop

A local marketplace and discovery platform designed for Tanzanian university students.

The platform helps students discover:

- Products
- Shops
- Accommodation
- Services
- Promotions

The goal is simple: Make it easier for students to find what they need around their campus.

### Deni Kiganjani

A lightweight business tool designed to help small businesses manage customer debts digitally.

Instead of relying on notebooks, businesses can keep track of:

- Customers
- Outstanding debts
- Amounts owed
- Payment promises
- Payment history

Built with the needs of small businesses and entrepreneurs in mind.

### Campus Delivery

Exploring software solutions for campus-based food delivery, including:

- Hostel delivery
- Group orders
- Shared delivery costs
- Vendor management
- Real-time order updates
